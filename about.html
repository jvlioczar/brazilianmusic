About the catalogue

A catalogue of Brazilian music for outsiders, and for insiders too.

What it is

Brazilian Music is a bilingual catalogue of Brazil's music for newcomers and for Brazilians alike. Instead of opinion, cards: every genre, artist, song, album and instrument gets a short, objective, findable entry.

How the content is organised

Everything starts from the genres. Brazilian music is divided into families and subgenres, each with its origin, decade and where to start listening. Artists, songs and albums link to them, and every person has one page that brings all their roles together. Around them, cards on instruments, awards, festivals, TV shows and a glossary give the context of the scene. You can come in from any end, a genre, a name or a song, and follow the links to the rest. Tiers S, A and B mark each artist's relative importance within their field: S for legends, A for giants, B for references.

Limits

A catalogue is a selection: no list can cover all of a country's music, and this one keeps growing, with new artists, songs and albums. Follower and sales figures change; where they appear, they date from the time of collection.
