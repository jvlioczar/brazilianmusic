Elis Regina

Type Solo artistFamily MPBDebut 1961State Rio Grande do SulTier S · Legend

The "Pimentinha": MPB's greatest interpreter; "Arrastão", "Águas de Março", "Como Nossos Pais".

About

Solo artist from Rio Grande do Sul, on record since 1961. Listed in the MPB family, under Festival-era MPB. Ranked among the catalogue's legends (tier S). Has 6 songs in the timeline and 6 essential albums.
