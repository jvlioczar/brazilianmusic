Brega, Calypso and Sounds of Pará

Since 1800Where Recife, Belém and inland BrazilSubgenres 8Artists 54

'Brega' was a critics' insult for the popular romantic music of Waldick Soriano, Odair José and Reginaldo Rossi; it became a genre name. In Pará, brega met carimbó and guitarrada and produced lambada, Banda Calypso and the tecnobrega of Belém's giant sound systems.

If you like…

If you like cumbia, retro kitsch or working-class party music, brega and tecnobrega will surprise you.
