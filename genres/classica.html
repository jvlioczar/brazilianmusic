Classical Music

Since 1760Where Rio and São PauloSubgenres 5Artists 21

Carlos Gomes premiered 'O Guarani' at La Scala in 1870; Heitor Villa-Lobos made the 'Bachianas Brasileiras' the most-performed Brazilian classical work in the world. Nelson Freire, Guiomar Novaes and Bidu Sayão were performers of global reputation, and OSESP is the reference orchestra.

If you like…

If you like Bartók, Stravinsky or concert music with folk roots, Villa-Lobos is the starting point.
