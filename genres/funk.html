FNK

Brazilian Funk

Since 1989Where Rio de Janeiro, then São PauloSubgenres 10Artists 119

Nothing to do with American funk: funk carioca was born at 1980s dance parties out of Miami bass and found its Portuguese voice with DJ Marlboro (1989). It moved through melody, proibidão, tamborzão, São Paulo's ostentação and 150 BPM; today 'baile funk' is exported by Anitta, Kevin o Chris and São Paulo's mandelão.

If you like…

If you like hip-hop, dancehall, reggaeton or club music, funk is the Brazilian beat that will get you.
