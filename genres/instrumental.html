Hermeto Pascoal, Egberto Gismonti, Airto Moreira and Naná Vasconcelos are revered by world jazz; Azymuth became a DJ cult; Toninho Horta and Hamilton de Holanda take Brazilian guitar and mandolin to international stages.

If you like…

If you like jazz, fusion or ECM, Brazil has one of the richest instrumental catalogues on the planet.
