RAP

Rap and Hip-Hop

Since 1988Where São Paulo, then Rio, Brasília and FortalezaSubgenres 3Artists 106

Brazilian hip-hop grew up in black dance parties and at São Paulo's São Bento metro station. Racionais MC's gave the genre its masterpiece in 1997; in the 2010s Emicida, Criolo and Djonga took rap to the centre of culture, and Matuê made trap the most-streamed sound among the young.

If you like…

If you like conscious hip-hop, boom bap or trap, Brazilian rap has direct equivalents.
