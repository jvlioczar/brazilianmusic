RAP

Rap and Hip-Hop

Since 1988Where São Paulo, then Rio, Brasília and FortalezaSubgenres 4Artists 120

Brazilian hip-hop grew up in black dance parties and at São Paulo's São Bento metro station. Racionais MC's gave the genre its masterpiece in 1997; in the 2010s Emicida, Criolo and Djonga took rap to the centre of culture, and Matuê made trap the most-streamed sound among the young.

If you like…

If you like conscious hip-hop, boom bap or trap, Brazilian rap has direct equivalents.

Slam and spoken word

Since 2008 · 3 artists

Spoken poetry as a competition, born from hip-hop: in 2008 Roberta Estrela D'Alva founded ZAP! (Zona Autônoma da Palavra) in São Paulo, Brazil's first slam. Since 2014 SLAM BR has brought together poets from across the country and sends its winner to the Slam World Cup in Paris. Mel Duarte.
