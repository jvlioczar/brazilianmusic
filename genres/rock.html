Brazilian Rock

Since 1955Where Rio, São Paulo, Brasília and the whole countrySubgenres 28Artists 471

It began with rock'n'roll covers in 1955, became Jovem Guarda in the 1960s, psychedelia with Os Mutantes, and exploded in the 1980s with the 'BRock' of Legião Urbana, Titãs and Paralamas. It has export-grade metal (Sepultura, Angra), hardcore, manguebeat, emo and a vigorous indie scene.

If you like…

If you like rock in any form, there is a Brazilian equivalent here: from post-punk to stoner, from metal to shoegaze.
