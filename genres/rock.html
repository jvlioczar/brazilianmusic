Brazilian Rock

Since 1955Where Rio, São Paulo, Brasília and the whole countrySubgenres 29Artists 492

It began with rock'n'roll covers in 1955, became Jovem Guarda in the 1960s, psychedelia with Os Mutantes, and exploded in the 1980s with the 'BRock' of Legião Urbana, Titãs and Paralamas. It has export-grade metal (Sepultura, Angra), hardcore, manguebeat, emo and a vigorous indie scene.

If you like…

If you like rock in any form, there is a Brazilian equivalent here: from post-punk to stoner, from metal to shoegaze.

Fock

Since 1998 · 5 artists
Fusion genre: rock + funk carioca

Rock and metal with the beat and vocal style of funk carioca, sometimes with rap. The mix began in Porto Alegre in the late 1990s with Comunidade Nin-Jitsu ('Detetive', from the album Broncas Legais, 1998), DeFalla and Ultramen. In the 2020s it got a name: fock, as MC Taya called it, and metal mandrake, the movement of the new heavy scene. Lvcas turns funk hits into metal.
