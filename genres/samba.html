Samba

Since 1860Where Rio de JaneiroSubgenres 14Artists 110

Heir to the samba de roda of Bahia's Recôncavo, carried into the homes of Rio's 'Bahian aunts' in the early 20th century, samba is the rhythm the world associates with Brazil. 'Pelo Telefone' (1917) is the first registered samba; from there came the samba schools, samba-canção, partido-alto and, in the 1980s, pagode.

If you like…

If you like communal music, percussion and lyrics that tell everyday stories, start with Cartola, Paulinho da Viola and Zeca Pagodinho.
