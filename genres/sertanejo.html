Sertanejo

Since 1929Where Rural São Paulo, Minas, Goiás and ParanáSubgenres 5Artists 89

Brazil's most-streamed genre today was born as caipira music in 1929, with viola duos singing in thirds. It modernized with Chitãozinho & Xororó, became romantic pop in the 1990s and, after 2005, the 'universitário' of Jorge & Mateus, Luan Santana and Marília Mendonça took over the charts.

If you like…

If you like country, especially Nashville country-pop, sertanejo is its Brazilian cousin.
