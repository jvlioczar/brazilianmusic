Sertanejo

Since 1929Where Rural São Paulo, Minas, Goiás and ParanáSubgenres 9Artists 92

Brazil's most-streamed genre today was born as caipira music in 1929, with viola duos singing in thirds. It modernized with Chitãozinho & Xororó, became romantic pop in the 1990s and, after 2005, the 'universitário' of Jorge & Mateus, Luan Santana and Marília Mendonça took over the charts.

If you like…

If you like country, especially Nashville country-pop, sertanejo is its Brazilian cousin.

Forronejo

Since 2001 · 2 artists
Fusion genre: forró + sertanejo

Forró and sertanejo on the same groove. Osmar & Osmailson took it nationwide in 2001; Wesley Safadão made it massive from 2015 on.

Osmar & OsmailsonWesley Safadão

Funknejo

Since 2012 · 3 artists
Fusion genre: funk carioca + sertanejo

Funk carioca beats such as tamborzão with the melodies and duos of sertanejo universitário: João Lucas & Marcelo, Cácio & Marcos, Dennis DJ.

Cácio & MarcosJoão Lucas & MarceloDennis DJ

Pagonejo

Since 2018 · 4 artists
Fusion genre: pagode + sertanejo

Pagode meets sertanejo: 'Atrasadinha' (2018), by Felipe Araújo and Ferrugem, topped the charts; Alexandre Pires released the album Pagonejo Bão (2025).

Alexandre PiresFerrugemOba Oba Samba HouseFelipe Araújo

Rocknejo

Since 2018 · 2 artists
Fusion genre: rock + sertanejo

Sertanejo with distorted guitars and rock drums. The name spread with the Rocknejo carnival bloc, founded in Belo Horizonte in 2018, and with party bands; Edson & Hudson and Lauana Prado are examples of the mix.

Lauana PradoEdson & Hudson
