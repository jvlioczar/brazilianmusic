Brazilian music producers

Who is behind the records: producers, arrangers and beatmakers. The first names are featured; each name opens the person's page.

Producers 44

Rick Bonadio

Mamonas Assassinas, Charlie Brown Jr., Rouge, LS Jack, NX Zero, Fresno, Tihuana, CPM 22, Manu Gavassi, Vitor Kley; founder of Midas Music, judge on Popstars and Ídolos

Liminha

Os Mutantes, Rita Lee, Raul Seixas, Titãs, Barão Vermelho, Ira!, Ultraje a Rigor, Gilberto Gil; sophisticated arrangements in rock and pop

Mario Caldato Jr.

Beastie Boys, Jack Johnson, Marcelo D2, Seu Jorge, Nação Zumbi, Marisa Monte, Mallu Magalhães; international career in the US

Paulo Anhaia

Oficina G3, Velhas Virgens, Charlie Brown Jr., Los Hermanos, CPM 22, Rouge, Fresno, NX Zero; frequent partner of Rick Bonadio

Alexandre Kassin

Caetano Veloso, Los Hermanos, Vanessa da Mata, Mallu Magalhães, Clarice Falcão; member of +2, experimental approach

Arnaldo Saccomani

Ronnie Von, Os Mutantes, Dominó, Sorriso Maroto, Pixote, Tim Maia, Rita Lee, Fábio Júnior; career since the 1960s and TV judge

Rodrigo Castanho

Tihuana, Supla, Ultraje a Rigor, CPM 22, Fresno, Hateen, NX Zero, Planta & Raiz

Rafael Ramos

Alceu Valença, Elza Soares, Pitty, Titãs, Dead Fish, Black Pantera; founder of Deckdisc

Papatinho

ConeCrewDiretoria, Gabriel o Pensador, Marcelo D2, Black Alien, Anitta, MC Guimê, Orochi; Rio rap beatmaker

Carlos Eduardo Miranda

Raimundos, Skank, O Rappa, CSS, Gaby Amarantos; influential in 1990s rock and TV judge

Tadeu Patolla

Charlie Brown Jr., Strike, Biquini Cavadão, Jorge Ben Jor, Pedra Letícia; discovered Charlie Brown Jr.

Dudu Marote

Skank, Jota Quest, Pato Fu, Adriana Calcanhotto, Emicida, BaianaSystem, Lagum

KondZilla

MC Guimê, Kevinho, Kekel, Lexa, MC Fioti, MC Lan; founder of KondZilla Records and funk's viral videos

Jean Dolabella

Milton Nascimento, Lenine, Ana Carolina, Paul Di'Anno, Mike Patton; Pitty's drummer and former Sepultura

Dudu Borges

Jorge & Mateus, Bruno & Marrone, Michel Teló, Luan Santana, Gusttavo Lima, Cristiano Araújo; architect of the sertanejo universitário sound
