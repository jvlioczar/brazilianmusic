252 singers

Brazilian singers

The voices that defined Brazilian music, from the radio era to 2020s pop, including the singers of the big bands. The first names are featured and the rest are in alphabetical order; each name opens the person's page.

Singers 252

Elis Regina

The "Pimentinha", regarded as MPB's greatest interpreter: "Como Nossos Pais", "Águas de Março".

Milton Nascimento

A unique falsetto and timbre; Clube da Esquina and "Travessia".

Gal Costa

The voice of Tropicália, from "Baby" to "Chuva de Prata".

Tim Maia

The "Síndico", the voice of Brazilian soul.

Ney Matogrosso

The countertenor of Secos & Molhados and of the country's most theatrical solo career.

Elza Soares

A husky voice and scat, from samba to the experimental; named singer of the millennium by the BBC in 1999.

Maria Bethânia

A dramatic interpreter; the first Brazilian woman singer to pass a million records, with "Álibi".

Cássia Eller

The deep voice of 1990s rock and MPB.

Marisa Monte

The great MPB voice of the 1990s and 2000s; Tribalistas.

Renato Russo

Legião Urbana's singer and lyricist.

Ivete Sangalo

Axé's biggest star, from Banda Eva to her solo career.

Andre Matos

Singer of Viper, Angra and Shaman; a reference in melodic metal.

Cazuza

Voice and lyrics of Barão Vermelho and a solo career: "Exagerado", "Ideologia", "O Tempo Não Para".

Chorão

Singer and lyricist of Charlie Brown Jr., the voice of 1990s and 2000s skate rock.

Marília Mendonça

The "queen of sofrência", the voice of feminejo: "Infiel", "Todos os Cantos".
